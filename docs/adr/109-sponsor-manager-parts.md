# ADR-109 · Sponsor management: the sponsors list, the hero preview and the layout notes (S7-T11, part B2)

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-09
- Related: ADR-105 (the server side), ADR-106 (the picture slot and the form), ADR-099 (the Hero page boxes), ADR-045 and ADR-038 (how the banners are chosen and shown), FR-ADM-11, DEC-15
- Plan approved by the owner on 9 Nov 2026 ("approved", all seven recommended answers). S7-T11 is built in four changes: the server side (ADR-105); the picture slot and form parts (ADR-106); these parts (B2); the sponsor manager on the Hero page (C).

## Decision

- **Parts first, in the Design Lab, three entries on a new board, "Sponsor list"** (`/design-system/sponsor-manager`). The Sponsors board (the slot and the form) is already about 18,000 px high at 1440 px; adding these would double it, so they get their own board, as the Site content parts did. No page is connected here. None of the parts holds state of its own beyond the preview's Desktop or Phone choice; the page keeps the data and passes the words, already translated.
  - **`SponsorsManager`** (domain): the Sponsors section of the Hero page: heading, the line "Shown in this order, one after another.", Add sponsor, then the sponsors as a table from 1024 px of room and as cards below (the existing `ResponsiveTable`), with the name and a monogram, the status (Active, Inactive, Needs images), whether the Strip and Panel formats are Ready or Missing, the Up and Down arrows, Edit and Delete, and the count line. The first sponsor's Up and the last one's Down are off; every button says which sponsor it is for. Nothing yet shows a message with its own Add sponsor. A row being saved has all its buttons off.
  - **`SponsorHeroPreview`** (domain): the live preview with the first sponsor's banner in it: a Desktop or Phone switch, the layout's name and the sponsor that is showing, and the drawing (the banner under the search in Search Desk; beside it in Split on a desktop and under it on a phone), at the slot's true shape taken from the public site's own `SLOT_RATIOS`. When no sponsor can be shown it says so and draws the search card alone, as visitors see it. It reuses `SiteHeroPreview`, which gains two optional props (`banner`, `stacked`); without them it draws what it drew before, so the Hero page's existing preview and pictures are unchanged.
  - **Two small additive props:** `SiteContentLayout` gains `after` (a full-width section between the form and the save bar, where the approved screen puts the sponsors list) and `SiteHeroFields` gains `layoutNote` (what the page shows under the two layout cards). Neither changes anything when left out.
- **The notes use the public site's own rules.** `heroCoverage` (`src/lib/sponsor-coverage.ts`, with tests) asks the same `selectSponsors` that the home page uses, so "2 of 3 sponsors don't have the Split format" is true to what visitors get, including a picture the slot cannot fit. Only active sponsors are counted: one switched off is not expected to show. The preview shows the first sponsor that can show and "first of N active" where N counts those that can.
- **A Delete button is added to each row.** The approved screen shows the "Delete Sample Builder?" question (state `confirm-delete`) but no button that opens it. A trash icon button beside Edit, named in full ("Delete Sample Builder"), opens it, as on the Units list. The question's words are the screen's: "This removes the sponsor and its images. Visitors stop seeing it straight away. This can't be undone."
- **"Needs images" means no ready format** (neither a strip nor a panel with both pictures). The screen's "Phone image missing" case cannot happen: a format is both pictures or neither (ADR-105), so the Strip and Panel columns say only Ready or Missing.
- **The "Booking: …" line under each name is left out**, as decided in ADR-105 (no such field is stored); the name gets a two-letter monogram only.
- **Not built here, on purpose:** adding, editing, saving and uploading (part C), and any scheduling, billing, report or click count (not built, as in the plan).

Nothing is stretched."), "Live layout: …", the two layout notes, "Delete sponsor", "Cancel". Differences and additions, for your confirmation:

- The line under the heading is "Shown in this order, one after another." (the owner's decision 2, replacing "There is no automatic rotation").
- New: "Inactive" (a sponsor switched off), "Unnamed sponsor" (one saved before the editor had a name box), the Delete buttons' names ("Delete Sample Builder"), the preview switch's name ("Preview size"), and "Delete Sample Builder?" for the question's title (the screen's own).
- The layout notes say "N of M sponsors don't have the Split format" (or "the Search Desk format") and count only active sponsors; for one sponsor it reads "1 of 3 sponsors doesn't have …" and "It won't be shown …". The "No sponsor can be shown in Split" note names the Search Desk fall-back as on the screen; in Search Desk it says "the homepage without a banner".

## Consequences

- No migration, package, gate, environment or server change. A twelfth Design Lab board with three entries: its page, its row in `docs/ux-reference.md`, its registry entry, the registry test's list and the two e2e lists that name the boards (the index test now says twelve). Three new pictures, taken in CI at 390, 820 and 1440 px and committed as baselines after your approval. The board is called "Sponsor list" because a longer name ("Sponsor manager") made the twelfth link wrap onto a fourth line at 390 px, which changed the height of every board's picture at that width; the shorter name still fits on the third line, so no existing picture changes.
- Rollback: delete the two parts, the helper and its test, the demos, the board with its page, row and registry entry, the e2e board names, the two small props, the tests, the registry test change and this ADR. Nothing else uses them yet.
