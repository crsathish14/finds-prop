# ADR-044 · Compare (S4-T02)

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-05
- Related: FR-PUB-10, ADR-039 (the cards' Compare checkbox), ADR-042 (results), ADR-043 (property page), `docs/ux/final-ux/screens/compare.html`
- Plan approved by the owner on 5 Nov 2026 ("All recommended"), together with the sponsors (ADR-045), in one change. Place search (S4-T05) was left out by the owner.

## Decision

- **The selection is in the visitor's browser** under one key, `findsprop:compare:v1`: up to three homes, signed out or in, kept across reloads (FR-PUB-10). Each entry is `{ id, title, cover }`: the public id, plus what the bar needs to draw it (the title for the remove button's name, the cover picture's address), so the bar never fetches anything. `parseSelection` is tolerant: text that is not JSON, an entry with no valid public id, a repeat and anything past the third are dropped, a bare id is accepted, and a cover is drawn only when it is an http(s) address or a rooted path.
- **One external store behind one hook** (`useCompare`, `useSyncExternalStore`): the cards' checkboxes, the property page's button, the bar and the comparison all read the same list, in this tab and, through the `storage` event, in every other. Where the browser refuses to keep it (private mode, storage blocked) the list lives in memory for the visit. On the server and on the first browser render the list is empty, so a page and its first hydration agree; the selection fills in afterwards.
- **The toggles:** `CompareCardToggle` (the checkbox in a card's foot, on the results and the homepage sections) and `CompareButton` ("Add to compare" under the title of a property page). At three, every other toggle switches off with "You can compare up to 3 homes"; a chosen home's own toggle stays on so it can be removed. The card draws its chosen ring from the toggle's own state (`has-[button[data-compare][aria-pressed=true]]`), so the card stays a server component.
- **The bar** appears after the first home is chosen: three slots with the pictures and a remove button each, "N of 3 selected" with what to do next, Clear all, and Compare now, which stays off until two are chosen and then leads to `/compare?ids=a,b,c`. It is drawn on the homepage and the results (above the phone's bottom bar) and beside a home's page from 1024 px (a phone has the sticky price bar there), and not on the comparison itself or anywhere else (`compareBarMode`, a pure rule). It is mounted once, in the public layout.
- **The comparison** (`/compare?ids=`): the address is the comparison, up to three public ids. The homes are read the way the property page reads them (`getProperty`, as a visitor, cached for minutes), so the two cannot disagree. **A home that is gone** (sold, rented, withdrawn, never listed) is left out silently, and an address that asked for it, for a repeat, for a fourth or for something that is not an id is sent to the address of what is shown (a shared link keeps working with the homes that remain). A read that fails throws, as the property page's does, and the route's error screen answers. Fewer than two homes is the UX's empty state; loading has a skeleton.
- **The table** (UX): a label column and a column per home (picture, tags, title as a link, price, View home, Remove), then Overview (price, price per sq.ft for a sale only, kind), Facts (locality, area, bedrooms, bathrooms, furnishing) and one row for every amenity any home has, with Yes or Not listed. What a home did not give is "Not listed", never blank. On a phone the table scrolls sideways and the label column stays. The table is the pure `CompareScreen` over plain columns (`CompareColumn`), so the Design Lab draws it with sample columns; the server builds the columns from the homes (`toCompareColumn`).
- **Remove with Undo:** Remove takes the home out of the address (a replace, not a history step) and out of the visitor's own selection if it is there, and an Undo toast puts both back. A comparison opened from a shared link does not touch the visitor's own selection until they remove something.
- **Not built:** Save (S5-T01), a share button, comparing homes of different listing types differently (a sale and a rent are shown side by side, each with its own price line).
- **Design Lab:** a new **Discovery** board (the compare bar, and the comparison in three states), so the Domain board, which is already near its 30-second test limit, does not get heavier. The board list in the Lab tests and `docs/ux-reference.md` are updated.

## Consequences

- Not tried against real homes: the hosted dev database needs secrets, so the read is tested with a stub and the table with sample columns; the bar and the selection are tested in a browser with the selection put in storage.
- `src/lib/short-id.ts` now holds the public-id check that the property address and the selection share.
