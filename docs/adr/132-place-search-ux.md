# ADR-132 · Place search: the screens and parts (Design Lab only)

- Status: Proposed. The parts are built and shown in the Design Lab; the owner approves the pictures from CI. Nothing is wired to a page, a route or the database in this change.
- Date: 2026-11-17
- Related: ADR-131 (the adapter), ADR-012 and ADR-042 (search), ADR-060 (listing submit), ADR-099 and ADR-102 (the localities editor), DEC-19, DEC-35, FR-GEO-01 to FR-GEO-03, UX addendum ADD-07 and ADD-08, `OLA_MAPS_PLAN.md` (outside the repository) sections 4.3, 4.6 and 5
- Owner answers of 17 Nov 2026: ADD-07 approved as drawn; everything in one pull request; the radius choice, the "Also near" section and the distance on a card may be designed from existing parts and approved from the CI pictures; no map anywhere for now; a listing location is required with an area fallback; the admin card shows calls to Ola Maps only.

## Decision

- **Place suggestions (ADD-07, approved as drawn).** `Combobox` gains optional place rows (round pin, the name with the typed letters marked, the rest of the address under it, an arrow on the chosen row), a panel footer, notes for before three letters and for no match, unavailable and too many searches, a keyword row that keeps the typed text, grey loading rows, and `defaultOpen` for the Lab. A combobox that passes none of these is unchanged. `usePlacePanel` holds the words of each state and `PlaceAttribution` the footer: "Powered by Ola Maps" and the OpenStreetMap licence link, which the Ola terms require to stay visible. `SearchCard` passes the panel through (`localityPanel`); choosing the keyword row searches the typed text.
- **Deviations from the drawing, for the owner to confirm:** the "Ola Maps" credit is plain text, not bold (no rich-text message exists in `en.json`); the note for one or two letters says "Keep typing and suggestions will appear." (the drawing says "One more letter", true for two letters only); the full-screen sheet for phones is **not built in this change** (the panel opens under the field at every width; the sheet is listed in the pull request as left to do).
- **Search within (new, not in the addendum).** `FilterFields`, `FilterRail` and `FilterSheet` take `radiusChoices` (2, 5, 10 and 15 km; `RADIUS_CHOICES_KM`) and draw a "Search within" chip row in the style of the bedroom chips, with 5 km chosen when none is set; and `placeField`, a slot for the place search box in place of the Locality list, with "Tamil Nadu only for now." under it. `FilterValues` gains an optional `radiusKm`.
- **Distance on a card (new).** `PropertyCard` takes an optional `distanceText` ("2.4 km away", or "About 6 km away" for a home placed only by its area), shown in bold after the place.
- **Also near (new).** `NearbySection` is a labelled section under the main results: "Also near {place}", a note ("3 more homes within 15 km, nearest first."), and the same grid as the results. The page draws nothing when there are no homes further out.
- **Location field (new; ADD-08 without its map).** `LocationField` is the address step with suggestions and no map: the address box, the Ola Maps line with the Privacy Policy link, "I can't find it. Choose the area instead", an area chooser, a "Location found" card with Change (exact or approximate), a warning when suggestions are unavailable (the area is asked for, so posting never stops), and "Add a location to continue." as an alert. The pin, its three map states and the map wording of the drawing are not drawn. The line about what visitors see follows the wording B of the drawing, which matches DEC-35 as built: the public point is rounded to about 100 metres.
- **Location prompt (new).** `LocationPrompt` is an info `Alert` on My listings: "Add your location to appear in nearby searches", with the number of listings that have none and an "Add location" link.
- **Admin card (new).** `PlacesUsageCard` shows our own count of calls to Ola Maps: today, this month, problems in 30 days, a line per day with its Chart / Table switch, where the calls came from and how they ended. It shows calls only: no cost, no allowance and no credit balance. It reuses `StatTile`, `ChartCard`, `LineChart`, `ChartTable` and `BarList`.
- **New wording** is in `en.json` under `domain.places`, `domain.nearby`, `domain.locationField`, `domain.locationPrompt`, `domain.placesUsage`, plus `domain.propertyCard.distanceAway` and `distanceApprox` and `domain.filters.regionOnly` and `radius`. The pull request lists every new line for the owner to approve.

## Consequences

- The Design Lab gains entries "Place suggestions" and "Nearby homes" (Domain board), "Location field" (Seller board) and "Place search usage" (Admin board). Their screenshot baselines do not exist yet: the first CI run fails those pages, and the baselines are committed after the owner approves the pictures.
- Wiring is later work: the route, the usage count and the search box (plan PRs 3 to 4), the distance sort and "Also near" on `/properties` (5 and 6), the dashboard card (6b), the post form step and the prompt (7a to 7c), the admin pickers (8). Until then none of these parts is used by a page.
- The place panel assumes the lookup answers with a name and a short address for each suggestion (`label`, `detail` from ADR-131). The Ola answer carries no pincode field, so the pincode in the drawing's second line appears only when it is part of the address text.
