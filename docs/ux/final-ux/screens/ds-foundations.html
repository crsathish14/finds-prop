Accessibility

Measured contrast (WCAG 2.1)

Small copper text must use --c-accent-text (#9A4A20). The brand copper #B4592A is 4.42:1 on ivory, so it is for button fills and display-size text only. Disabled controls are exempt from contrast rules but are never the only cue.

Headings step down at 1024 and 768 px (display 58 / 46 / 30). Layouts reflow rather than scale, and nothing relies on a fixed width below 1024 px. Touch targets are at least 44 px; the only smaller controls are 36 px dense buttons on desktop.

Interaction

Focus ring and motion

Focused chip

One ring everywhere: 3 px forest outline with a 2 px offset (2 px inside fields, where the border also darkens). It is an outline, so it survives high-contrast mode. On dark surfaces the ring is light copper. Motion is 120 to 200 ms ease-out and is removed when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
