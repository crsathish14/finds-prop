Design system

Feedback and states

How the product answers back: alerts, toasts, confirmations, empty and loading states, validation and failures. Messages say what happened and what to do next, in plain words, and name the thing they refer to.

Alerts

Inline messages

Enquiry sentThe owner can now see your details and message.
Sign in to contact the ownerWe will take you straight back to this home.
Edits go back for approvalThe project is hidden until it is approved again.
We couldn't send your enquiryYour details are still here. Try again in a moment.
Builder accounts are created by invitation only.

Errors use role="alert" and are announced; success and info use role="status". An alert always has an icon and a title, so colour is never the only signal.

Toasts

Brief confirmation after an action

Marked as soldIt no longer shows in search results.
Removed from compare
Couldn't saveCheck your connection.
PlacementBottom centre on desktop; above the bottom bar on mobile. One at a time.
TimingStay 6 seconds, pause while hovered or focused, and never carry the only copy of something important: errors also stay inline on the page.
UndoOnly where the action can really be undone (removing from compare). Deleting a listing cannot be undone, so it is confirmed first instead.

Confirmations

Dialogs

Delete this listing?

"Independent villa with private garden and terrace" will be permanently deleted, along with any enquiries on it. This can't be undone.

Mark this home as sold?

It will be removed from search results. You can't undo this.

DoName the thing (“Delete this listing?”), say the consequence, and repeat the verb on the button. Put the safe choice first in reading order and give it initial focus when the action is destructive.
BehaviourEscape closes. Focus is trapped inside and returns to the button that opened it. On phones, dialogs stay centred; forms use a bottom sheet instead.
Don'tUse “Are you sure?”, “OK / Cancel”, or confirm actions that are easy to reverse.

Validation

Errors next to the field, summary for long forms

2 things need attentionAdd a project name and a slug before saving.
Project name is required.
WhenOn submit, and on leaving a field that was edited. Never while typing the first time.
WhereUnder the field, red text with an icon, linked with aria-describedby. Forms longer than one screen add a summary and move focus to the first error.
WordsSay how to fix it (“Enter a phone number with 6 to 15 digits”), not what the rule is called.
SuccessConfirmed on the page that matters (a success panel, or a toast plus the new state), not a generic banner.
