Design system

Sponsored banner container

Advertisers supply a finished banner image. Find Prop owns the frame, the label and the controls; the advertiser owns everything inside the image. All banners on this page are fictional samples with placeholder copy. Campaign logic, booking, billing and reporting are out of scope and listed in design-handoff.md.

File weight and accepted file types are a development decision (suggestion: JPG, PNG, WEBP up to 150 KB).

Fitting

Never distorted

Matches the slot fills the frame (object-fit: cover)
Near miss (970 × 120 for a 970 × 90 slot) shown whole on a neutral ground (contain)
Extreme mismatch rejected when supplied, never shown
Example: a 4:3 photo (1.33 : 1) for a 10.8 : 1 slot would shrink to a thumbnail. It fails the ratio check and the campaign falls back to the no-sponsor layout.
RuleIf the image's ratio is within about 8 % of the slot's, it fills the frame and may lose a sliver at the edges. If it is further off but still within about 30 %, it is shown whole, centred, on the parchment ground (letterboxed). Anything further is rejected when supplied.
Safe zoneKeep headline, logo and button at least 5 % inside every edge, because phones crop slightly differently.
LimitsNever stretched. Never enlarged beyond 1.5× its supplied size. Text smaller than 11 px at display size is the advertiser's risk and is flagged in review.
