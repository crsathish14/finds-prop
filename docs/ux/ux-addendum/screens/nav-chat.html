ADD-17 · Navigation

Navigation with the AI chat on

AI Search stands out in the header, the phone bottom bar gets a centre AI tab with Post next to it, and the saved-homes heart is gone because Saved homes live under the account menu. With the chat off, the old design is used exactly as approved.

  • Removed, only while the chat is on: the heart icon with its count in the header (signed in) · the Saved tab in the phone bottom bar · the raised Post (+) in the centre of the phone bar
  • Changed: header "AI Search" is a copper-tint pill with a sparkle (a tint, not a copper button, so each page keeps its single copper action) · phone bar is Home, Search, AI (centre, highlighted), Post, Me
  • Kept unchanged: Buy · Rent · Projects · Contact · Post property · Sign in · account menu (it already lists Saved homes with its count) · mobile menu drawer, including "Your account" and "Saved homes" · footer
  • When the chat is off: the old design only. No AI pill and no AI tab, the heart is back in the signed-in header, and the phone bar is Home, Search, Post (raised), Saved, Me, exactly as approved and as in ADD-10. Same components, one flag.

Header · visitor, desktop

AI Search is a copper-tint pill. Hover darkens the tint. Below 1024 px the links live in the menu drawer, as approved.

Header · on the AI page

The current page is shown with a thin copper-text outline on the pill (the other links keep the approved underline). The label stays "AI Search".

Header · signed in, account menu open

No heart (chat on). Saved homes (with its count) is the first row under Dashboard in the account menu, exactly as approved.

Phone bottom bar · chat on

Home · Search · AI · Post · Me. AI is the centre tab; Post is a normal tab beside it. The tab label is "AI" (its name for screen readers is "AI Search").

Phone bottom bar · on the AI page

The AI tab shows the current page with a ring on its disc and the approved copper mark above it.

Phone bottom bar · chat off

The old design only: Home · Search · Post (raised) · Saved · Me, exactly as approved. The header with the chat off is the one in ADD-10 (no AI link, heart when signed in). Nothing changes.

What does not change

  • Because the old design stays whenever the chat is off, the approved screens and the ADD-10 boards need no change. This navigation applies only while the chat flag is on.
