ADD-17 · Navigation

Navigation with the AI chat on

AI Search stands out in the header, the phone bottom bar gets a centre AI tab with Post next to it, and the saved-homes heart is gone because Saved homes live under the account menu.

  • Removed: the heart icon with its count in the header (signed in) · the Saved tab in the phone bottom bar · the raised Post (+) in the centre of the phone bar while the chat is on
  • Changed: header "AI Search" is a copper-tint pill with a sparkle (a tint, not a copper button, so each page keeps its single copper action) · phone bar is Home, Search, AI (centre, highlighted), Post, Me
  • Kept unchanged: Buy · Rent · Projects · Contact · Post property · Sign in · account menu (it already lists Saved homes with its count) · mobile menu drawer, including "Your account" and "Saved homes" · footer
  • When the chat is off: no AI pill and no AI tab (as ADD-10). The phone bar is Home, Search, Post (raised, as approved), Me. Saved stays removed. Same components, one flag.
