What does carpet area mean?
findsprop's AI assistant

Carpet area is the floor space you can actually use inside a home, measured wall to wall. It leaves out the thickness of the walls, balconies and shared spaces such as lobbies and stairs.

Super built-up area adds a share of those shared spaces, so it is always larger than the carpet area.
